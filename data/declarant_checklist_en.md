# Checklist: authorisation as a CBAM declarant in Austria

As of 27.11.2026. The competent authority is the **Office for National Emissions Trading (Amt für den nationalen Emissionszertifikatehandel, AnEH)** at the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF):
cbam@bmf.gv.at, +43 (0) 50 233 560 555 (Mon–Thu 7:30–15:30, Fri 7:30–12:00). Customs questions: Austrian Customs Office (Zollamt Österreich).

> Preliminary check, not legal advice. Binding are Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/486 and the information from the AnEH.

## 1. Do I need the authorisation?

- [ ] Total net mass of all CBAM goods in the calendar year **above 50 t** (electricity and hydrogen: always) → authorisation needed. Since 1.1.2026, imports above the threshold require an authorisation (or an indirect customs representative who holds one).
- [ ] Below 50 t: state the exemption in the customs declaration. The BMF names TARIC code **Y137** for this, elsewhere **Y128**. Check with the AnEH or the customs office which code applies in which case.
- [ ] Alternative: appoint an **indirect customs representative** (freight forwarder) with a CBAM authorisation. Importers established outside the EU must do this.

## 2. Set up access

- [ ] **EORI number** (Austrian Customs Office)
- [ ] Create an **EU Login**, click the confirmation link (valid for 24 h), set a password, note the **"uid" code**
- [ ] **USP** (Business Service Portal, mein.usp.gv.at; sign in with ID Austria, FinanzOnline or via the tax office). The USP administrator grants the permission "Nationaler Emissionszertifikatehandel" (takes up to 60 minutes)
- [ ] In the USP tile "Nationaler Emissionszertifikatehandel" → register as "CBAM-Teilnehmer" (EORI, EU Login e-mail, phone)
- [ ] **E-mail the uid code and company name to cbam@bmf.gv.at**, wait for the AnEH to check it
- [ ] **NEIS registration**: decisions are delivered via the National Emissions Trading Information System

## 3. Application in the CBAM Registry (Authorisation Management Module, AMM)

Sign in: CBAM → Austria → Economic operator → EORI, then EU Login. Menu "Authorisations" → "New application".

- [ ] Master data: name, address, contact person, EORI, main activity in the EU
- [ ] Activity data: type of goods, **estimated quantity and value of imports** in the current and the next year, per Member State
- [ ] Declaration of honour: no serious infringements of customs, tax or criminal law in the last 5 years
- [ ] Financial and operational data: legal form, employees, year of establishment, VAT number, AEO status (number, if any), annual accounts
- [ ] Submit with "Proceed". The **application number** is assigned automatically; quote it in every enquiry

## 4. Documents (each not older than 3 months)

- [ ] **Extract from the fiscal criminal record** of the company (Finanzstrafregisterauszug, Office for Combating Fraud)
- [ ] **Tax clearance certificate** (Unbedenklichkeitsbescheinigung, form Verf 34a, tax office)
- [ ] **Criminal record extract** of the managing directors registered in the commercial register
- [ ] **Bank guarantee** using the BMF template (Bürgschaftserklärung) if the company was founded after 31.12.2023. Amount according to the guide: (certificates of the application year + the following year) × estimated certificate price; the guide assumes €80 per certificate
- [ ] **Proof of creditworthiness** (e.g. KSV), only if at least **6,000 t CO₂** from imports is expected in 2026 or 2027 and there is no AEO status

## 5. After the decision

- [ ] The authorisation takes effect upon entry in the CBAM Registry; you receive a **CBAM account number** (if a guarantee is required, only once it is registered)
- [ ] Report changes (quantities, representative, company data) via the CBAM Registry; this can trigger a new review
- [ ] Buy certificates for 2026 imports from **1.2.2027** on the central platform; first annual declaration by **30.9.2027**

## Note on the transitional rule

Anyone who had applied by 31.3.2026 could import without a valid authorisation until 27.9.2026; imports under TARIC code Y238 (with application number "APPL-AT-2025…") are possible only until 31.12.2026. This rule no longer applies to new applications — **apply before you exceed 50 t**.
